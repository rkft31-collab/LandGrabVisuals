Method. Identify the highest-authority qualifying Criterion 2 artifact, then code the substantive depth of that same artifact. Deeper language from a lower-level unit is treated as supplemental evidence rather than combined with a different artifact’s authority.

Reading the figure. Categories increase from left to right and bottom to top. These are ordinal positions only: adjacent categories are not assumed to be equally distant.
