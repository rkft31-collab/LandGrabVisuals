Criterion 3 · Ordinal matrix

Indigenous Student Support and Educational Infrastructure

Universities are positioned in ordered qualitative categories. Columns represent the breadth of Indigenous student-support infrastructure; rows represent Indigenous authority in curriculum and knowledge.

University placement Categories are ordered; labels X1–X4 and Y1–Y4 are identifiers, not quantitative measurements.

Method. Student-support breadth and curricular authority are coded separately. Non-tuition financial assistance may contribute to support breadth; full tuition waivers are excluded and coded under Criterion 4.

Reading the figure. Categories increase from left to right and bottom to top. These are ordinal positions only: adjacent categories are not assumed to be equally distant.

Interpreting Level 1. Level 1 may represent no qualifying institutional Criterion 3 remedy and can include isolated, general, or student-led activity.
