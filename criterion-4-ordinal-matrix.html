University abbreviation key

Criterion 4 distinguishes legal compliance from substantive repair. The existence of a NAGPRA policy, inventory, coordinator, or legally required consultation process does not alone constitute a high-level remedy. Higher placement requires evidence of sustained action beyond minimal compliance, material redistribution, meaningful Tribal authority, or structural transfer of land, resources, or institutional power. A low Criterion 4 placement does not establish legal noncompliance with NAGPRA.
