Holistic Institutional Response to Indigenous Land-Grant Dispossession

Institutional Recognition and Indigenous Infrastructure versus Material and Structural Repair

University response profileX1–X4 and Y1–Y4 are ordered category identifiers, not measurements, averages, or rankings.

Method. The holistic matrix does not average the four criteria or assign continuous scores. Horizontal placement synthesizes institutional recognition under Criterion 2 and Indigenous student and knowledge infrastructure under Criterion 3. Vertical placement synthesizes the material and structural remedies identified under Criterion 4. Criterion 1 functions as an absence condition rather than a continuous dimension. The resulting cells represent institutional response profiles, not numerical rankings.

Interpretive note. A university may therefore occupy a high position on one dimension and a considerably lower position on the other. This is intentional: symbolic recognition and Indigenous student infrastructure are analytically distinct from material redistribution, Tribal authority, and structural repair.
